How to play

1. Place your 5 ships on your board. Pick a ship from the list, then click a square. Use Rotate (or press R) to switch between horizontal and vertical, or click Place ships randomly.

2. Choose how tough the computer should be, then click Start battle.

3. Take turns firing. Click a square in Enemy Waters. A red ✕ is a hit, a white dot is a miss. After each of your shots, the computer fires back at your fleet.

4. Sink all 5 enemy ships before the computer sinks yours to win.
